J. Bowkett et al.

Closed-loop compliant motion and contact-rich control present distinct challenges in spaceflight robotics, where harsh thermal variations, extreme latency constraints, unmodeled environment compliance, and low-stiffness structural modes complicate interaction dynamics. This paper addresses the theoretical and practical obstacles to executing high-precision continuous force control on flight-representative robotic arms, detailing gravity compensation, wrench filtering, tare behaviors, and stability analysis.

Recommended citation: Bowkett J. et al. "Challenges in Closed-loop Compliant Motion Control for Planetary Robotics." *IEEE Aerospace Conference*, 2024.
