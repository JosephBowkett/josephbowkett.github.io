This paper presents the design, software architecture, and field demonstration of an end-to-end autonomous robotic surface sampling system developed for the Europa Lander mission concept. The autonomy framework integrates visual perception, workspace analysis, kinematic reachability, force/torque proprioceptive monitoring, autonomous science site targeting, fault detection and recovery, and closed-loop execution on physical flight-representative lander hardware deployed at Matanuska Glacier, Alaska.
